bus / dma_engine

Dma Engine Top

dma_engine with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map dma_engine_regs (u_regs, src/include/dma_engine_regs.sv, ID "DMAE", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic dma_engine (u_core, src/dma_engine.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from dma_engine_regs.json and the ports of dma_engine: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
