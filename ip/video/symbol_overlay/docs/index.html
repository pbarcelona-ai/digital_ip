Symbol Overlay Top

symbol_overlay with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map symbol_overlay_regs (u_regs, src/include/symbol_overlay_regs.sv, ID "SOVL", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic symbol_overlay (u_core, src/symbol_overlay.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from symbol_overlay_regs.json and the ports of symbol_overlay: regenerate after changing either. Clock - clk. Reset - synchronous active low rst_n.
