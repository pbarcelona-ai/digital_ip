Object Tracker Top

object_tracker with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map object_tracker_regs (u_regs, src/include/object_tracker_regs.sv, ID "OTRK", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic object_tracker (u_core, src/object_tracker.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from object_tracker_regs.json and the ports of object_tracker: regenerate after changing either. Clock - clk. Reset - synchronous active low rst_n.
