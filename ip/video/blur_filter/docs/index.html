Blur Filter Top

blur_filter with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map blur_filter_regs (u_regs, src/include/blur_filter_regs.sv, ID "BLRF", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic conv2d_filter (u_core, src/conv2d_filter.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from blur_filter_regs.json and the ports of conv2d_filter: regenerate after changing either. Clock - clk. Reset - synchronous active low rst_n.
