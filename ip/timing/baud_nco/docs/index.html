Baud Nco Top

baud_nco with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map baud_nco_regs (u_regs, src/include/baud_nco_regs.sv, ID "BNCO", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic baud_nco (u_core, src/baud_nco.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from baud_nco_regs.json and the ports of baud_nco: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
