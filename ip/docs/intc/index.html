peripherals / intc

Intc Top

intc with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map intc_regs (u_regs, src/include/intc_regs.sv, ID "INTC", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic intc (u_core, src/intc.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from intc_regs.json and the ports of intc: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
