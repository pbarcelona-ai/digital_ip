peripherals / spi_master

Spi Top

spi_master with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map spi_master_regs (u_regs, src/include/spi_master_regs.sv, ID "SPIM", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic spi_master (u_core, src/spi_master.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from spi_master_regs.json and the ports of spi_master: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
