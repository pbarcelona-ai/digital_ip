Frame Counter Top

frame_counter with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map frame_counter_regs (u_regs, src/include/frame_counter_regs.sv, ID "FCNT", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic frame_counter (u_core, src/frame_counter.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from frame_counter_regs.json and the ports of frame_counter: regenerate after changing either. Clock - clk. Reset - synchronous active low rst_n.
