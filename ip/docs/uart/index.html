peripherals / uart

Uart Top

uart with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map uart_regs (u_regs, src/include/uart_regs.sv, ID "UART", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic uart (u_core, src/uart.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from uart_regs.json and the ports of uart: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
