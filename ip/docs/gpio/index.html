peripherals / gpio

Gpio Top

gpio with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map gpio_regs (u_regs, src/include/gpio_regs.sv, ID "GPIO", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic gpio (u_core, src/gpio.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from gpio_regs.json and the ports of gpio: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
