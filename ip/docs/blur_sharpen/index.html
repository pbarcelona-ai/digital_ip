Blur Sharpen Top

blur_sharpen with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map blur_sharpen_regs (u_regs, src/include/blur_sharpen_regs.sv, ID "BLSH", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic blur_sharpen (u_core, src/blur_sharpen.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from blur_sharpen_regs.json and the ports of blur_sharpen: regenerate after changing either. Clock - clk. Reset - synchronous active low rst_n.
