Quad Dec Top

quad_dec with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map quad_dec_regs (u_regs, src/include/quad_dec_regs.sv, ID "QDEC", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic quad_dec (u_core, src/quad_dec.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from quad_dec_regs.json and the ports of quad_dec: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
