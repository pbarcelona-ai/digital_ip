peripherals / pcie_tl_ep

Pcie Tl Ep Top

pcie_tl_ep with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map pcie_tl_ep_regs (u_regs, src/include/pcie_tl_ep_regs.sv, ID "PCIE", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic pcie_tl_ep (u_core, src/pcie_tl_ep.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from pcie_tl_ep_regs.json and the ports of pcie_tl_ep: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
