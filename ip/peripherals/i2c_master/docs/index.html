peripherals / i2c_master

I2C Top

i2c_master with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map i2c_master_regs (u_regs, src/include/i2c_master_regs.sv, ID "I2CM", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic i2c_master (u_core, src/i2c_master.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from i2c_master_regs.json and the ports of i2c_master: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
