peripherals / spi_flash_ctrl

Spi Flash Ctrl Top

spi_flash_ctrl with its AXI4-Lite register interface, the block designs and testbenches instantiate: the register map spi_flash_ctrl_regs (u_regs, src/include/spi_flash_ctrl_regs.sv, ID "SPFL", VERSION and DATE at 0x00-0x08, the registers from 0x10) and the logic spi_flash_ctrl (u_core, src/spi_flash_ctrl.sv), joined by their register ports (cfg_* to the logic, sts_* back). Generated by ip/scripts/regmap_gen.py from spi_flash_ctrl_regs.json and the ports of spi_flash_ctrl: regenerate after changing either. Clock - aclk. Reset - synchronous active low aresetn.
